# Bolt.Earth vs Statiq, Tata Power EZ Charge, ChargeZone and Jio-bp pulse: Network Size, Business Models, Pricing, Funding and Financials in India's EV 

## Executive Summary

Bolt.Earth reports over 100,000 chargers across India and more than 361,000 active EV users. Bolt.Earth's total funding is listed at $29M by CB Insights, while an investment publication reports a fund raise of ₹437.50 Cr. Statiq's Indian entity saw revenue fall 40% to Rs 40.9 crore in FY24, and its FY25 losses rose 3.1X to Rs 44.52 crore. Tata Power EZ Charge reports 6,700+ public, semi-public and fleet charging points across 690+ cities and towns. ChargeZone uses a dealer-owned, company-operated model with a minimum investment of about ₹1 crore per highway station. Reliance BP Mobility Limited generated more than ₹1,000 Cr in revenue in the financial year ending 31 March 2026. Profit or loss figures for most competitors are not available.

## Bolt.Earth: network scale, funding and business model

**Confidence: LOW**

Bolt.Earth, formerly known as RevOS, was founded in 2017 and is based in Bengaluru, India. [Source: cbinsights.com, 2026-09-21, credibility: 0.46] Bolt.Earth has deployed over 100,000 chargers across India and serves more than 361,000 active EV users. [Source: clay.com, 2025-01-01, credibility: 0.26] Bolt.Earth offers standard and fast chargers for 2W, 3W and 4W vehicles, along with a Charger Management System and a mobile application for managing charging sessions. [Source: cbinsights.com, 2026-09-21, credibility: 0.46]

Bolt.Earth's platform lets users install, manage and access EV charging infrastructure, including home or personal parking installations for individuals and charging stations for businesses that can create additional revenue opportunities. [Source: ipoplatform.com, 2026-03-11, credibility: 0.57] CB Insights lists Bolt.Earth's total funding as $29M, with the last raise at $5M about two years earlier. [Source: cbinsights.com, 2026-09-21, credibility: 0.46] Clay.com states Bolt.Earth has raised $29 million across six funding rounds, with the latest being a Series A round involving three investors dated 01/01/2025. [Source: clay.com, 2025-01-01, credibility: 0.26]

An investment publication dated 25-Mar-2026 reports a Bolt.Earth fund raise of ₹437.50 Cr. [Source: ipoplatform.com, 2026-03-11, credibility: 0.57] Bolt.Earth's State of Charge 2026 report, based on its internal network telemetry, found charging demand accelerating in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities including Vijayawada, Rajamahendravaram and Thiruvananthapuram. [Source: cbinsights.com, 2026-09-21, credibility: 0.46] The report studied charging activity across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi-NCR, Mumbai and Kochi over FY24-25, FY25-26 and FY26-27 year-to-date. [Source: cbinsights.com, 2026-09-21, credibility: 0.46]

## ChargeZone: highway expansion, DOCO model and financing

**Confidence: LOW**

ChargeZone's DOCO (Dealer Owned, Company Operated) model has landowners, entrepreneurs, businesses and institutional investors provide capital, and in many cases land or property, while ChargeZone handles development, commissioning, technology integration, and operations and maintenance. [Source: markhub24.com, 2026-09-27, credibility: 0.66] ChargeZone is based in Vadodara, Gujarat, and is also present in the UAE. [Source: electrive.com, 2026-04-03, credibility: 0.58] As of a 2024 announcement, ChargeZone reported more than 3,200 charging points across 400-plus locations in India and the UAE. [Source: markhub24.com, 2026-09-27, credibility: 0.66]

A later report says ChargeZone has set up more than 15,000 charging points across more than 1,200 locations in India and the UAE. [Source: electrive.com, 2026-04-03, credibility: 0.58] ChargeZone plans to deploy over 1,000 fast-charging stations across major national highway corridors by FY2027. [Source: vahanbazaar.in, 2026-04-08, credibility: 0.38] Each highway station will deliver between 500 kW and 1.5 MW of charging capacity and requires a minimum investment of approximately 1 crore (10 million rupees), covering charging hardware, electrical infrastructure, civil work, and initial software and payment system setup. [Source: vahanbazaar.in, 2026-04-08, credibility: 0.38]

ChargeZone's franchise program materials reference a range from ₹25 lakh to ₹10 crore depending on station type. [Source: markhub24.com, 2026-09-27, credibility: 0.66] ChargeZone has tied up with the State Bank of India to help dealers secure financing for charging stations. [Source: electrive.com, 2026-04-03, credibility: 0.58] A 2024 press announcement put ChargeZone's total raised at $54 million, including a Series A1 round led by BlueOrchard Finance, while CB Insights records total funding of $86.43 million at Series A-III stage. [Source: markhub24.com, 2026-09-27, credibility: 0.66]

Disclosed ChargeZone investors include British International Investment, Macquarie Capital, and BlueOrchard, among others. [Source: markhub24.com, 2026-09-27, credibility: 0.66] **[UNVERIFIED]**

## India public EV charging: operators, counts, tariffs and benchmarks

**Confidence: MEDIUM**

A 2026 guide compares six charge-point operator networks that matter to private car owners in India: Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq, ChargeZone, Jio-bp pulse, Zeon and Bolt.Earth. [Source: evautoindia.co.in, 2026-09-09, credibility: 0.65] Jio-bp's standard public DC charging rate is Rs 24 per unit, and subscriptions bring it under Rs 21. [Source: evautoindia.co.in, 2026-09-09, credibility: 0.65] Bolt.Earth AC sockets are often priced at Rs 8 to 12 per unit. [Source: evautoindia.co.in, 2026-09-09, credibility: 0.65]

Public DC fast charging in India cost between about Rs 14 and Rs 24 per unit in 2026, depending on operator, state and charger power, while home charging cost about Rs 6 to 9 per unit. [Source: evautoindia.co.in, 2026-09-09, credibility: 0.65] Statiq counts chargers on its app, including partner units it does not own. [Source: evautoindia.co.in, 2026-09-09, credibility: 0.65] Tata Power counts stations, some with several guns, in its charger count. [Source: evautoindia.co.in, 2026-09-09, credibility: 0.65]

Rubix Data Sciences put India's total public charger count at 67,657 in August 2026 and found that 73 per cent of chargers are rated under 30 kW. [Source: evautoindia.co.in, 2026-09-09, credibility: 0.65] Maharashtra's latest tariff order set INR 9.50 per unit for high-tension and low-tension EV charging stations for multiple years. [Source: marqstats.com, 2026-03-28, credibility: 0.38] The MoP EV Charging Guidelines 2024 set a revenue-sharing model in which the agency owning the land receives Rs 1 per kWh of electricity used for charging, paid quarterly. [Source: chargedekho.in, 2026-09-25, credibility: 0.46]

## Gaps & Limitations

- Bolt.Earth funding is unresolved: CB Insights and Clay list $29M total, while an investment publication reports a ₹437.50 Cr fund raise. The timing of the latest round also differs between sources (about two years earlier vs 01/01/2025).
- Bolt.Earth revenue and profit or loss from the latest financials are not in the evidence.
- Statiq's FY25 revenue is not reported, and one source says FY25 numbers were undisclosed at the time of reporting while another cites an FY25 loss of Rs 44.52 crore. Its charger and city counts also vary between statements (10,000+ across 1,014+ cities vs 8,000+ across 100+ cities in 2025).
- Tata Power EZ Charge funding, revenue and profit or loss are not in the evidence, and its charging-point counts differ (6,700+ vs 5,500+) with different scopes and cities (690+ vs 700+).
- ChargeZone revenue and profit or loss are not in the evidence. Total funding is unresolved ($54M in a 2024 announcement vs $86.43M per CB Insights), as is network size (3,200+ points in 2024 vs 15,000+ points later).
- Jio-bp pulse profit or loss is not in the evidence. The only revenue figure is for Reliance BP Mobility Limited as a whole and may not isolate EV charging. Funding raised by Jio-bp is also not reported.
- Specific per-unit charging tariffs for Statiq, Tata Power EZ Charge and ChargeZone are not in the evidence; only Jio-bp, Bolt.Earth AC and market-wide ranges are available.
- Several sources are low-credibility or dated outside the last 24 months, and one Jio-bp item carries inconsistent dates.
